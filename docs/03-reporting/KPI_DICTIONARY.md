# KPI Dictionary

| KPI | Definition | Source | Frequency | Guardrail |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Physical progress | accepted measured quantity / approved quantity, weighted by value where approved | progress records | weekly/monthly | state weighting and exclusions |
| SPI | EV / PV | EVM snapshot | weekly/monthly | compare aligned cut-off dates |
| CPI | EV / AC | EVM snapshot | weekly/monthly | explain accrual completeness |
| Forecast at completion | selected EAC method with assumptions | cost forecast | monthly | show confidence and drivers |
| Schedule variance | forecast or actual date minus baseline date | schedule update | weekly | identify critical-path driver |
| Cost variance | forecast/actual minus baseline | cost forecast | monthly | separate approved change |
| High exposure | risk score/exposure above threshold | risk register | weekly | thresholds are configurable |
| Open NCRs | accepted? no; count of active NCRs | quality register | weekly | age and severity matter |
| Overdue HSE actions | due date passed and not verified closed | HSE register | weekly | escalate critical actions |
| Constraint clearance | cleared constraints / due constraints | look-ahead | weekly | include owner and date |
| Procurement readiness | approved/available items for next look-ahead | procurement register | weekly | ordered is not available |
| Handover readiness | accepted evidence items / required evidence items | handover register | monthly | distinguish physical completion |

KPIs are signals. They do not replace the underlying records or professional judgment.
